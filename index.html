The promise, exactly

These are the figures of the sample dossier we ran before writing this page — a real zone, Lyon (France), locksmiths, 25 listings read on 23 November 2026 between 10:26 and 10:52 in the morning, no Google account, no paid data source:

We do not sell “25 proofs”. In a typical zone of 25 listings, four to six hold up in front of Google, and the rest are arguments, not proof. A page promising 25 proofs out of 25 listings would be inventing them. You get the ranking of all 25, the argument for each one, and a real evidence sheet for the ones that hold — the first page of your dossier states those counts before anything else.

Two rules often sold with this kind of audit are not used here, because they cannot be measured honestly from public pages: the number of photos on a listing (Google does not expose a usable counter) and the date of the first review (the public list no longer offers an oldest-first sort — reaching the first review means scrolling the whole list). Selling you a verdict on either would be an opinion dressed as a measurement.

What this dossier does not do

  • It does not file anything for you. No report is submitted anywhere. The complaint goes from your account, in your name — that is also what makes it credible.
  • It promises no removal. Google decides, and only Google. What is sold is the evidence and the text, not the outcome.
  • It does not call anyone a fraud. A shared address can be a genuine shared workshop; a duplicated name can be a second branch. The dossier reports public facts, scores them, and quotes Google’s own rules. The judgement is Google’s.
  • It uses no private data. Only what any visitor sees on the public listing pages: display name, category, address, phone, website, rating, review count, opening hours.

Questions

Is this legal?

Yes. Every field in the dossier is on a public page that any visitor can open. Nothing is scraped from a private account, no Google account is used, no personal data is collected about individuals — only the business information a listing publishes.

Who buys this?

The local tradesperson who sees phantom competitors above his own listing — locksmiths, plumbers, heating engineers, garages — and the local SEO agency that maintains listings for several of them and needs a dossier per city.

Will my competitors be told?

No. Nothing is sent to anyone. You are the only recipient of the dossier; whether you file a complaint, and when, is yours to decide.

Can I ask for a different city or trade?

Yes — that is the product. One dossier covers one trade in one city. For several cities, buy one per city.

What if the dossier finds nothing?

Then it says so, with the ranking to prove it. A zone where all 25 listings look genuine is a real answer: it tells you where not to spend your time. You still receive the ranking.
